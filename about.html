About Us

We Don't Cut Corners.
We Coat Them.

Process Over Promises

Complete Crete Coatings was built on a simple idea: if you get the prep right, the floor takes care of itself.

We're based in Cookeville, Tennessee and we serve residential, commercial, and industrial clients across the Upper Cumberland and beyond. From two-car garages to 20,000 SF facilities — the standard doesn't change.

Every floor we install follows the same disciplined process. Evaluate the concrete. Prep it properly. Apply the right system. Protect it with a topcoat that performs. No shortcuts. No guessing.

Why It Matters

Most coating failures don't start at the surface — they start underneath it. Before any coating is applied, the concrete has to be mechanically prepped, corrected, and brought into the right condition for the system being used.

This step doesn't get much attention, but it determines whether a floor performs long term or starts showing issues months later.

When that discipline is in place, the finished floor isn't a gamble — it's a predictable work of art.

No Shortcuts

We don't skip steps to save time. Proper prep is non-negotiable on every single job.

Right System

We select the coating based on your space — traffic, moisture, use. Not whatever's cheapest.

Always Available

We pick up the phone. If you have a question about your floor, we're here.
