Services

The Right System
For Every Space

We don't do one-size-fits-all. Every floor gets the coating system that matches its use, traffic, and environment.

Most Popular

Garage Flake Systems

Full broadcast vinyl flake over a high-build base coat with a UV-stable topcoat. This is our bread and butter — 70% of our installs are garage flake. Durable, seamless, chemical resistant, and it transforms any garage into a finished space.

Best For

Residential garages, shop floors, utility rooms, basements

Install Time

2-3 days depending on size and prep — we never rush the process

Finish

Full broadcast flake, satin topcoat, seamless edge-to-edge coverage

Metallic Coatings

One-of-a-kind metallic epoxy systems that create depth, movement, and a truly custom floor. Every install is unique — you can plan, prep, and execute perfectly, but the final result is never identical to the last one. That's what makes them rewarding.

Best For

Showrooms, high-end garages, retail spaces, salons

Characteristics

High-gloss, reflective, organic flow patterns, completely unique each time

Note

These systems come with some risk — creative problem-solving is part of every install

Polyaspartic Systems

Fast-cure, UV-stable coatings that can be installed and returned to service the same day. When downtime costs money, polyaspartic is the answer.

Best For

Commercial spaces, restaurants, medical facilities, quick turnarounds

Cure Time

Walk-on in hours, full service same day or next day

Properties

UV-stable, chemical resistant, won't yellow or amber over time

Concrete Polishing

Mechanically ground and polished concrete for a clean, modern industrial look. No coatings — just the concrete itself, refined to a smooth, durable finish.

Best For

Warehouses, retail, restaurants, modern residential

Maintenance

Extremely low — dust mop and occasional damp mop

Finish Levels

Matte, satin, or high-gloss depending on your preference

Stain & Seal

Acid stains and concrete dyes that penetrate the surface for rich, permanent color. The concrete's natural character shows through for an organic, one-of-a-kind look.

Best For

Patios, showrooms, retail, restaurants, decorative residential

Color

Permanent — penetrates the concrete, won't peel or flake

Style

Natural variation, warm earth tones, or custom colors

Ceiling Coatings

Specialty coatings for exposed ceilings in garages and commercial spaces. A clean, finished ceiling ties the whole room together and completes the transformation.

Best For

Garages, workshops, commercial buildouts

Why

Finishes the space — most people forget the ceiling until they see it done

Options

Flat white, dark tones, or color-matched to your floor system
